ARCHITECTURE LESSON

Business problem: Stock-outs versus over-ordering

Inventory below a reorder point is not automatically a purchase requirement. Existing requisitions, purchase orders and approvals may already cover the shortage. Rex computes the uncovered need before asking an LLM to investigate.
