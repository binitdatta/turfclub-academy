ARCHITECTURE LESSON

Two kinds of agents — different authority

TurfBot · Interactive / Read-only

A human starts a question; LangGraph coordinates Claude and role-filtered GET tools. TurfBot cannot mutate the ERP. Responses arrive as Server-Sent Events and every lookup is recorded.

AI Workforce · Scheduled / Controlled writes

A scheduler starts an employee run. Claude investigates and proposes. Python policy code validates the proposal, and a separate executor performs narrow ERP writes, subject to approvals.

The critical enterprise distinction is language-model reasoning versus authority to execute. Both systems use LLM reasoning, but neither delegates its security policy to the model.
