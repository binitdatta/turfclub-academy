The big picture

Enterprise Architecture: From Racecourse to General Ledger

See how club staff, TurfBot, Keycloak, shared master data, operational ERP services, accounting events and MySQL work together. This architecture connects the business process to the technical implementation without losing the boundaries between modules.

Secured from the start

Keycloak, OAuth 2.0/OIDC and role-specific access protect human users, service calls and TurfBot tools.

Business processes, connected

Operational services exchange shared business context and live validations without writing into one another's tables.

Financial integrity

Transactional accounting events carry business activity into General Ledger posting and finance workflows.

Operations

Racing, betting and prizes, members and hospitality, supply. Modules check each other live over /api/v1 where a rule needs a current answer.

Accounting event bus

Every money movement is written to integration.accounting_event_outbox in the same transaction and relayed to the GL, which applies one of 58 posting rules.

How Independent ERP Modules Collaborate

Services never write into each other's tables. They cooperate in four ways, each chosen for what has to be true at the moment of the action.

MechanismUsed whenExamplesIf the other side is down
Shared master data
enterprise_core		 Everyone must mean the same person, place or day An owner, a jockey, a supplier and a member are one party; meetings only on open calendar days Read-only views on each service's own connection: nothing to wait for
Live REST calls
/api/v1, client credentials		 A rule needs the other module's current answer Horse eligible today? Jockey licensed on race day? Member in good standing? Book this receipt into stock The action is refused with "… is not reachable at http://localhost:81xx". Nothing half-done
Accounting events
outbox → relay → GL		 Money moved and the books must follow Entry fee, ticket sold, goods received, bill to member account, bank charge The module's action still succeeds; its event waits as PENDING and posts when the GL is back
AR import
posted events → customer invoices		 A module charged someone who now owes the club Membership fees, registration, licence and entry fees, member charges, event bookings Imports catch up on the next run (every minute); nothing is imported twice

Follow Three Real-World Business Transactions

Race day

steward · tote · accountant
  1. Race Operations enters a horse after live checks with the Horse Registry and Participants; entry fee to the largest owner.
  2. Wagering sells tickets; pools close themselves at the off.
  3. Race Operations certifies the result.
  4. Wagering settles: takeout, dividends, breakage.
  5. Prizes splits the purse by race-day ownership and pays winning tickets.
  6. The GL's pool liability 2210 and dividends payable 2200 return to zero.

Procure to pay

storekeeper · buyer · tcadmin · accountant
  1. Inventory's reorder report shows oats below the reorder point.
  2. Purchasing raises the requisition; a second user approves the order.
  3. The goods receipt books stock in Inventory (Dr 1300 / Cr 2110).
  4. AP invoices from the PO with a three-way match and clears GRNI into 2100.
  5. AP pays from the bank (Dr 2100 / Cr 1100).
  6. Treasury reconciles the statement line to the GL line.

Member on account

memberdesk · hospitality · accountant
  1. Membership activates a member; the first year is invoiced.
  2. Hospitality checks standing live before the reservation.
  3. Dinner is billed to the member account, with tax.
  4. AR imports both as invoices to the member.
  5. AR records the payment into the bank.
  6. Treasury reconciles it; AR equals GL 1200.
